OUR STORY

Every Ton Recovered Is a Resource Preserved.

IronCore Recycling began as a family-operated ferrous collection depot dedicated to honest weights and dependable payouts for local machine shops.

Today, our 14-acre paved facility operates hydraulic shears, automated eddy-current separators, and a dedicated commercial roll-off fleet—serving industrial plants with the same transparent handshake we started with.
