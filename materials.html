METALLURGY GUIDE

Ferrous vs. Non-Ferrous

MAGNETIC // HIGH VOLUME

Ferrous Metals

Iron-based alloys prized for structural strength. Sorted via overhead electromagnets and sheared for electric-arc steel mills.

  • Heavy Melting Steel (HMS 1 & 2)
  • Cast Iron & Machinery Blocks
  • Structural Beams & Plate
NON-MAGNETIC // HIGH VALUE

Non-Ferrous Metals

Corrosion-resistant alloys commanding premium per-kilogram payouts. Graded inside covered bays using XRF analyzers.

  • Copper, Brass & Bronze Alloys
  • Extruded & Cast Aluminium
  • 304/316 Stainless, Lead & Zinc
