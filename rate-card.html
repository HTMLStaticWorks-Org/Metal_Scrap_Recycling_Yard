VALUATION FACTORS

How Your Rate Is Determined

01. Material Quality

Clean, dry metal free of plastic, wood, or oil earns top-tier pricing.

02. Alloy Purity

Verified via handheld XRF spectrometers for exact stainless & copper grades.

03. Quantity & Tonnage

Bulk commercial loads (5+ tons) qualify for volume bonuses and bin haulage.

04. Grade Preparation

Pre-sorted, sheared, or baled loads unlock higher net payouts.

05. Market Variation

Scrap values track global LME and mill indices, updated daily at our desk.

06. Zero Contamination

Drained fluids and minimal moisture prevent weight deductions at the scale.

YARD CHECKLIST

Prepare Your Scrap for Top Rates

Book Weighbridge Slot
01
Separate Ferrous

Keep magnetic steel & iron apart from copper, brass, and aluminium so loads aren't downgraded to mixed iron.

02
Strip Clean Copper

Separating bare bright #1 copper wire from insulated cable unlocks significantly higher per-kilogram payouts.

03
Drain Fluids

Empty cutting oils, coolants, and water from turnings and machinery blocks to avoid tare deductions.

04
Bring Trade ID

Have commercial dispatch notes or company ID ready at Gate B for fast weighbridge ticketing and settlement.
